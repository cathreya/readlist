PDFs & ebooks

  1. Select the passage in Preview, Kindle, or the Firefox PDF viewer.
  2. Copy it with ⌘C.
  3. Open this page and press ⌘V.
  4. Press ⌘↵.

Notes vs cards

⌘⇧K opens the card editor. ⌘⇧L opens the annotation popup and appends the highlight + your note to Writing/Reading/<Title>.md. ⌘⇧E asks the LLM to explain the passage. Right-clicking a selection also offers Annotate in Obsidian and Explain with LLM. They never share content: the note keeps the passage and source; the card keeps the question and answer.

Shortcut

On the web, ⌘⇧K captures a card, ⌘⇧L opens the annotation popup, and ⌘⇧E explains the passage. Change them in the userscript if they collide with a site.
